# Greener Space Planner

A web app that helps community organizations plan a greenhouse for fresh produce.

An organization answers a few questions about its site (space, budget, sunlight, water, electricity, season and crops). The app compares three greenhouse options, picks the best fit, and builds a plan with a layout, a harvest calendar and a costed shopping list. After setup, the site team can log harvests and deliveries, and the totals update automatically.

Built for Trent University's Innovation Bootcamp 2026 (Agriculture and Ag-Tech stream) by Team WHI.

**Live demo:** https://bellomk77.github.io/greener-space-planner/

## How it works

1. **Your site:** enter the site details. The three options update as you type.
2. **Compare:** Seasonal, Extended season and Winter-ready, each with setup cost, running cost, food per year and harvest months. Each option is checked against the budget, power and water on site.
3. **Your plan:** layout, harvest calendar, shopping list and an expert approval step.
4. **Harvest:** log harvests and deliveries and see weekly totals.

The best fit is picked with simple, transparent rules: budget, power and water each score 2 (fine), 1 (warning) or 0 (problem), and the option that matches when the site wants to grow gets 3 extra points.

## Run it

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Notes

This is a prototype. Prices, yields and harvest records are sample data, not real quotes. A real plan would be reviewed by a greenhouse expert before anything is built.
